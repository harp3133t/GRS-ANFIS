Purpose. This table package replaces the prior Gisette placeholder coverage rows with numeric rerun results where the completion artifacts exist. It keeps mixed results visible instead of turning every row into a positive narrative.

Key caveats. Gisette lambda/seed rows are fixed-fold seed reruns, the Gisette sequential row is a fixed-fold schedule comparison with a mixed outcome, and the Gisette correlated-feature row remains a representative top-100 subset stress test.
